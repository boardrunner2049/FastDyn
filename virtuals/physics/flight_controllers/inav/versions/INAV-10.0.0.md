# INAV 10.0.0 rehosting record

Firmware: INAV 10.0.0 development source at `d0c611e040887940226c9536bc84a3795ef9e800`, MATEKF405 (STM32F405). This is the version declared by that source, not a verified 10.0.0 release tag. The included ELF and its exact checksum are pinned in `configs/inav_matekf405.toml`.

## Challenges and resolutions

| Challenge and observed failure | Resolution | Evidence and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| STM32 oscillator/PLL ready bits are absent in QEMU. Firmware stalled in `systemInit`, before the scheduler. | A firmware-specific PC modifier skips the oscillator/PLL polling and retains the rest of `systemInit`. INAV's own SysTick and the FastDyn clock continue to run. | Guest reached the scheduler and executed the control loop. The modifier is tied to the included ELF. |
| Flash-backed EEPROM initialization failed because the emulated board does not implement STM32 flash programming. INAV's initial 8 KiB RAM configuration store was too small for its settings. | Build with `CONFIG_IN_RAM` and a 16 KiB EEPROM. Include `common/utils.h` for the RAM streamer's `ZERO_FARRAY`. | Settings initialize and the guest proceeds; settings are volatile and reset every run. |
| The firmware cannot detect or read a physical MPU6500 over the absent board SPI interface. | Identify the pinned MATEKF405 MPU bus device, simulate its identity and 14-byte sensor burst, and leave INAV's real driver, alignment, filtering, and estimator active. | Integration test reaches the MPU driver and reads level acceleration; other peripherals are not generally emulated. |
| The FMU uses body FRD, m/s² and rad/s; INAV's raw MPU values use 2048 counts/g, 16.4 counts/(degree/s), a +1 g stationary convention, and the board applies `CW180_DEG`. Incorrect signs or applying the rotation twice would tilt the estimator. | Negate specific force for INAV, invert the board's 180-degree yaw before the firmware applies it, convert units and saturate to signed 16-bit. | Unit tests cover rest, signed axes, FLU/FRD equivalence and saturation; an integration test checks the actual guest's filtered level acceleration. This does not establish alignment at all attitudes. |
| Default unity-gain, zero-offset accelerometer and compass settings are interpreted as uncalibrated by the embedded firmware. | For the ideal simulated sensors, mark accelerometer and compass calibrated using the same convention as INAV SITL, retaining zero offsets and unity gains. Run the normal gyro startup calibration. | Guest memory shows gyro calibration completed. Bias, noise and real six-position/magnetometer calibration are not validated. |
| No physical magnetometer, barometer or GPS was available. Their representations and update paths differ from ArduPilot. | Use INAV's fake magnetometer/barometer/GPS interfaces, with gauss × 1024, pressure in Pa, temperature in centidegrees, coordinates × 10⁷, and altitude/velocity in cm and cm/s. GPS fix and accuracy are synthetic. | Deterministic FMU test verifies magnetic field, pressure, temperature and coordinates in guest memory. FMU WGS84 altitude is not geoid-corrected. |
| INAV's default mixer contained zero motors; a direct copy of ArduPilot motor channels would not establish the correct body moment signs. | Initialize four INAV mixer entries from the Copter FMU's FLU motor-moment map, converting pitch and yaw to FRD. | Guest initializes four motors at 1000 µs disarmed; the real Copter FMU test arms and climbs more than 1 m. Long-duration stability and all-axis response remain untested. |
| INAV had no configured receiver. Arming by changing state directly would bypass firmware checks. | Feed validated local UDP frames into INAV's MSP receiver; configure AUX1 as arm switch and preserve INAV's receiver timeout and failsafe. | Tests reject an oversized frame, arm through the receiver, apply throttle, and observe receiver-loss failsafe. |
| Initial sensor and motor callbacks ran without advancing the FMU, so PWM changes did not reach the plant. | Advance the FMU every 1 ms using QEMU virtual time without injecting a duplicate SysTick. | A deterministic FMI test verifies PWM reaches the FMU; the real Copter FMU test verifies takeoff and receiver-loss failsafe. |
| Hook PCs and guest structure offsets depend on the firmware build, compiler and layout. Reusing the 10.0 profile on another ELF can write the wrong memory. | Pin the ELF SHA-256 and refuse a mismatch. Source patch, upstream revision, toolchain flags and build script are recorded beside the firmware. | A clean rebuild succeeded, with matching key symbol addresses but a different ELF digest. Every new version needs independent symbol, DWARF and runtime verification. |

## Remaining work

Telemetry and mission upload are not implemented. The real-model check demonstrates a climb of at least one metre and receiver-loss failsafe, not a sustained stable flight, mission, or full sensor calibration campaign. Two pre-existing FastDyn host-analysis unit tests fail because their FreeRTOS fixture lacks the expected `_start` alias and zero-size functions.
