# Historical host-cache recipes on the fixed FirmBOLT guest

Six of seven historical host-cache recipes materialized on the canonical FirmBOLT guest. The B4 validator rejected an uncertified x86 `bh` register operation at a specific translated block. Its linked rejection records the exact proposal and failed symbolic equivalence check. The eligible-recipe policy was fixed before this supplemental job. A missing or unresolved recipe would have blocked calibration.

On the separate Unix-documentation confirmation, it beat the same fixed guest’s ordinary cache in 12 of 12 paired blocks, with a median paired gain of 1.278× and one-sided p = 0.00108. Median editing latency fell from 51.455 to 40.142 ms. The ordinary and historical selected arms respectively added median 57 and 56.5 late translations from fresh copies of a training-only 3,511-record inventory.
